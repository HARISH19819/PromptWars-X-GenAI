## 📌 Executive Summary

Every year, millions of engineering students face a fragmented placement preparation journey: jumping blindly between YouTube playlists, LeetCode, aptitude PDFs, resume scanners, and random job portals. **Nothing communicates with each other.**

**Placement360 AI solves this fragmentation.** It provides each candidate with a dynamic **Placement Twin** that continuously evaluates readiness across 7 weighted dimensions, prescribes high-impact 15-minute micro-actions, stress-tests knowledge in real-time AI and peer environments, and directly bridges preparation with verified job market demand.

## 🧩 Key Capabilities & Features

### 1. 🎯 Dynamic Placement Twin & Multi-Factor Readiness Dial
Tracks candidate readiness in real-time across 7 scientifically weighted dimensions:
* **Technical Skills (25%)**: Aggregated from diagnostic test history and hands-on skill verification.
* **Target Role Alignment (15%)**: Alignment benchmark for roles like ML Engineer, SDE, Data Scientist, and Backend Developer.
* **Adaptive Assessments (15%)**: Dynamic score based on calibrated difficulty tests.
* **AI Mock Interview Arena (15%)**: Real-time performance during Project Defense and STAR rounds.
* **Group Discussion Fluency (10%)**: Peer collaboration, PREP structure, and clarity scores.
* **Verbal & Written Communication (10%)**: Speech articulation, filler-word suppression, and conciseness.
* **Resume ATS Match (10%)**: Keyword density, quantified project impact, and formatting hygiene.

$$\text{Readiness} = \sum (\text{Dimension Score}_i \times \text{Weight}_i)$$

---

### 2. ⚡ "Next Best Action" (NBA) Prescription Engine
* Eliminates decision fatigue by diagnosing the single highest-impact bottleneck.
* Automatically prioritizes critical gaps (e.g., if SQL JOIN score is below 50%, an interactive 15-minute challenge is prioritized before algorithmic sessions).
* Adapts dynamically as students complete modules, tests, or mock interviews.

---

### 3. 🎙️ AI Project Defense & Mock Interview Arena
* **Simulated Panel Pressure**: Interrogates candidate choices (e.g., *"Why did you use Adam optimizer over SGD with momentum in your PyTorch project?"*).
* **Native Voice Speech Recognition**: Built-in STT (`webkitSpeechRecognition`) transcribes candidate spoken answers in real-time.
* **Filler-Word Detection**: Pinpoints speech habits (`um`, `like`, `basically`, `you know`) and scores delivery confidence.
* **Methodology Evaluation**: Evaluates behavioral questions using the **STAR** framework (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
* **Follow-up Generation**: Generates contextual counter-questions based on candidate responses.

---

### 4. 👥 Live Peer Group Discussion (GD) Rooms
* **Interactive Collaboration**: Built-in WebRTC & Jitsi Meet peer rooms for instant video/audio collaboration.
* **Custom Scheduling**: Support for custom Google Meet and Zoom sessions with live status tracking.
* **AI Indicator Analysis**: Transcribes live discussion audio and evaluates candidate speech against the **PREP** framework:
  * **P**oint (Clear stance)
  * **R**eason (Logical rationale)
  * **E**xample (Data, real-world case study)
  * **P**oint (Concise takeaway)
* Turn-taking metrics, interruption frequency, and speaking time percentage tracking.

---

### 5. 📊 Adaptive Assessment Diagnostic Engine
* Dynamically calibrates test difficulty based on the candidate's real-time accuracy.
* Immediate micro-gap diagnosis (e.g., identifying confusion in database indexing vs. full table scans).
* Instant recalibration of the candidate's Placement Twin upon test completion.

---

### 6. 📄 Resume Intelligence & ATS Studio
* Instant parsing of raw resume text, extracting technical competencies, project metrics, and contact data.
* Role-specific keyword density and missing critical skill gap reports.
* 1-click integration with single-column, ATS-friendly LaTeX resume standards.

---

### 7. 💼 ZyncRole AI: Verified Live Job Intelligence
* Aggregated fresher and entry-level engineering roles across **LinkedIn, Google Careers, Amazon Jobs, Internshala, Unstop, Indeed, and Wellfound**.
* Direct, verified deep links to official company careers portals.
* **2-Way Market-to-Learning Loop**: If market demand for a skill (e.g., Docker or FastAPI) increases, the platform automatically queues targeted micro-learning modules onto the candidate's dashboard.

---

### 8. 📈 Longitudinal Trajectory & Growth Tracking
* Visualizes skill compounding from baseline diagnostic state to placement-ready state.
* Historical progression graphs showing before-and-after improvements across technical and behavioral rounds.
* One-click AI growth trajectory recalibration.
